Logo round 2

Built on what kids actually live with at school: the fence, the filter, the boredom. Each one is about quietly getting out, not shouting about it. Rough drafts, drawn by hand in code. The hoodie preview is there because if it doesn't look good small on a chest, it's not the one.

1The hole in the fence

Every school has one: the gap in the chain-link where people slip out. A fence with a hole peeled open, orange showing through. It says "we got out" without saying anything. Small, symbolic, works as a patch on a hoodie the way the best streetwear marks do.

2The tag

"recess" written like a tag on a bathroom stall or the back of a desk. This one is the strongest answer to "it feels AI": the best version is literally you writing it with a Sharpie, scanned in. That's how Stüssy started. Shown here with a stand-in handstyle font.

3The bored face

The face everyone makes in 4th period. Drawn like a margin doodle: half-shut eyes, flat mouth, one orange drop of sweat. Deadpan is how this generation jokes. It can become the mascot: it reacts in games, it shows up on loading screens, it gets merch.

4Fence and tag together

The full lockup: the fence mark as the icon, the tag as the name. The fence lives on the app icon and the chest. The tag lives on the site header and the back print.
