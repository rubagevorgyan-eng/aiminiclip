The swing, four ways

Same idea: the R never gets off the swing. Each take puts it on a different kind of screen. The small square is the app icon.

A. 8-bitPixel letters and a pixel swing that moves in choppy sprite frames, like it's running on a cartridge.
B. Vector arcadeThin outline letters like old vector arcade screens, and the R leaves ghost trails as it swings.
C. GlitchSolid heavy letters. The faster the R moves, the more it splits into red and blue, like a screen that can't keep up.
D. LED boardThe whole logo lit up on a dot-matrix board, the same one as the PA ticker. Every frame is redrawn dot by dot.
