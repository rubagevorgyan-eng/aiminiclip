Logo moments

Animated ideas for both names. Every tile loops or replays. Think of these as the logo's personality: the still version is the last frame.

Homework Games

1. Burn itNotebook paper, corner catches and burns, the ember edge keeps glowing. Your idea, built out.
2. Crossed out"homework" in pencil, a red pen line kills it, "games" gets scribbled in above. The name tells the joke.
3. Graded GGThe teacher's red pen circles your grade: GG. Every gamer knows it, every kid knows the red circle.
4. The dog ate itThe oldest excuse. A chomp takes a bite out of the logo, crumbs fall. The bite is the icon.
