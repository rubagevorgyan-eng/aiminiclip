How to play

One character goes on the block at a time, in a random order. Players bid with their $20. When the bidding stops, the host hits Sold. Once a player has five characters, they're out of the bidding.

Every card is a version of a character (Gojo in Hidden Inventory and Gojo at Shinjuku are different cards). A match draws one version per character: the strongest one allowed in your mode.

Stars come from the character's VS Battles Wiki tier: Tier 10 is 1★ Human, 9 is 2★ Fighter, 8 is 3★ Elite, 7 is 4★ Master, 6 is 5★ Legendary, 5–3 is 6★ Mythic and 2–0 is 7★ God Tier. That's this game's conversion, not an official VS Battles rating, and VS Battles is a community wiki, not official canon.

Power (1–100) places the tier inside its star's band, then speed, durability, hax, resistances, regeneration, intelligence and stamina separate characters on the same tier. Open View stats on any card to see the numbers and the source.

Anime + Manga is the default and uses every canon character and form. Anime only drops characters the anime hasn't shown and scores the rest on their animated forms.

The team with the highest total power wins. If it's a tie, the strongest single character decides it, then whoever has more money left.
