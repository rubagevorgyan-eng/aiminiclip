$20 Best Anime Character

by Ruben · Anime · 2 players and a host, one screen

$20BEST ANIME CHARACTER

Twenty bucks each. Five slots. One character on the block at a time, anywhere from a weak human to a god. Whoever builds the strongest team wins.

$20 EACHMAX 5SKIPS 3MASTER 5

How to play

One character goes on the block at a time, in a random order. Players bid with their $20. When the bidding stops, the host hits Sold. Once a player has five characters, they're out of the bidding.

Every character has a power score out of 100, ranked on one ladder across all seven shows by what they can destroy and who beats whom on screen. Stars are just bands of that score (60–74 is 5★, 75–89 is 6★ and so on), and each band splits into Low, Mid and High, so two 5★ characters still show who's stronger.

The team with the highest total power wins. If it's a tie, the strongest single character decides it, then whoever has more money left.

Bid+$1, +$2, +$5 or everything you've got. You can't raise your own bid.
Skip3 per player. Throws the character out, but only before anyone bids on it.
Master skip5 for the host, kept apart from player skips. Works any time.
GiftThe host hands the character to either player for free.
