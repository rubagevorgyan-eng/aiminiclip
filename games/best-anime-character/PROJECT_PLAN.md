## Character eligibility
Canonical anime, manga and manhwa characters, transformations, feats and abilities (latest officially released
forms, awakened forms, manga/manhwa-exclusive characters and forms, anime-original canon). Excluded: fan-made,
unofficial or hypothetical forms, leaks, non-canonical crossovers, films/games/GT/live action (origin whitelist).
Anime only = versions with anime status `shown` or `anime-only`; everything else (including `unknown`) is excluded,
and detailed VS stats stay hidden during play. Only `verified` versions are playable unless the setup's "Include
unreviewed characters" is on. One version per canonical character per match.

## Power-scaling method
Sources: VS Battles Wiki is the primary structured reference
([Tiering System](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Tiering_System), [Attack Potency](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_Potency),
[Speed](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Speed), [Standard Format](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_Format_for_Character_Profiles)),
using each profile's **key** for the exact version. It is a community wiki, not canon: entries keep
`stats` (VS Battles assessment), `canon.refs` (chapter/episode citations found in the profile) and `game` (our score) apart.
Official source material is the fallback when a profile is missing, outdated, disputed or ambiguous. Never invent stats,
profiles, URLs or verification results; missing values stay `null` and show "Not available".

**Seven stars** (game conversion, `config.stars`; same criteria for every universe, never popularity or in-series rank):

| Star | Rarity | VS Battles tier | Power band | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Human | 10 | 1–14 | #9098A5 |
| 2 | Fighter | 9 | 15–29 | #58B87B |
| 3 | Elite | 8 | 30–44 | #55A5EE |
| 4 | Master | 7 | 45–59 | #B18AF4 |
| 5 | Legendary | 6 | 60–74 | #EBA34D |
| 6 | Mythic | 5, 4, 3 | 75–89 | #E96878 |
| 7 | God Tier | 2, 1, 0 | 90–100 | #E8CB79 |

**Manual review** (status `needs-review`, reviewer sets `review.tier` + a note): Tier 11, Unknown, Varies, no parsable
tier, Tier-11 tokens. Ranges ("7-A to 6-C") use the lower bound; hedged tokens ("likely/possibly/at most X") are ignored
and the strongest unhedged tier is used; both are flagged. Verified entries record the flags in `review.notes`.

**Power score** (deterministic, `engine.score`; identical inputs → identical output, covered by tests):
`power = lo + round(span × (0.8 × position + 0.2 × secondary))`, clamped to the star's band.
- `position` = the tier's place among that star's subtiers (0..1), plus half a subtier step for "+", "at least" or an
  unquantified "higher". Tier is the foundation; secondary stats can move a character at most 20% of the band
  (≈3 points), so they never cross a star and only reorder near-identical subtiers.
- `secondary` = weighted mean of the factors that exist (missing ones are skipped and weights renormalised; never zero):
  combat speed 0.25 (rank vs. the star's expected speed; travel speed ignored), durability 0.15 (only a penalty when
  durability is below the AP tier, to avoid double-counting AP), hax 0.20 (Σ config hax weights / 3), resistances 0.10
  (count / 6), regeneration 0.10 (VS ladder), intelligence 0.10 (VS ladder), stamina 0.05, limitations 0.05 (manual grade).
  AP is never a secondary factor; abilities never raise the tier.
- Appearance probability is uniform per character (one version each) and never depends on power.
- **Migration from the old system**: stars used to be bands of a hand-set score (with Low/Mid/High). Stars now come
  from the tier number, and every legacy entry was re-scored with the formula from its stored tier, with neutral
  secondary (0.5). Examples (old → new): Netero 42 → 54 (4★), Sukuna 41 → 54 (4★), Kenpachi 70 → 76 (6★),
  Escanor 87 → 84, Renji 90 → 85 (6★; no God-tier characters exist in these seven series under this mapping),
  Julius 60 → 62 (manual override dropped), Tazuna 2 → 8.

## Character schema (`data/characters/<series>.js`, validated by `engine.validate`)
`id` (entry id `char--version`, stable) · `charId` (shared by versions; one per match) · `name` · `alt[]` · `series`
(id → `config.series` name/colour) · `form` · `continuity` · `medium` anime|manga|manhwa · `anime` shown|not-shown|anime-only
· `img` · `desc` (short original text, nullable) · `vsb{url,title,key,revid,revTimestamp}` ·
`stats{source, tier, ap, speed{combat,reaction,travel}, durability, durabilityTier, striking, lifting, stamina, range,
intelligence, experience, abilities[], hax[], resistances[], regeneration, weaknesses}` (all nullable; `[]` = none,
`null` = unknown) · `canon{refs[], feats}` · `review{status, date, by, tier?, notes[], grades{limitations}}`.
Computed at load (not stored): `game{star, rarity, color, power, tier, plus, position, secondary, factors, flags}`.
Statuses: `verified` (tier + key checked against the cited revision, other stats auto-extracted), `imported`,
`legacy` (tier only), `needs-review`, `excluded`.

## Rule history: auction semantics before Step 4 (recorded before any change)
- **Bid** (player p, amount a): allowed only if p's team isn't full, p isn't already the high bidder, a ≤ p's money and
  a > current high bid. Buttons offer high+1, high+2, high+5 and ALL (= p's whole balance). No money moves on a bid.
- **Sold** (host): only when a bid exists. High bidder pays the bid, gets the character; next character.
- **Player skip**: needs ≥1 of p's own skips and **no bid yet** this round; costs 1 of p's skips; the character is
  discarded (never returns). Allowed even when p's team is full (kept; noted as an open decision).
- **Master skip** (host): needs ≥1 shared master skip; works any time, also mid-bid; the pending bid is voided
  (nobody pays); costs 1 master skip only; the character is discarded.
- **Gift** (host → p): p's team must not be full; costs p $0; any pending bid is voided; next character.
- **End**: both teams full, or every character has been drawn. **Winner**: higher total power; tie → stronger
  single character; tie → more money left; else dead even.
- Verified problems found: a Sold/Gift click can land on the *next* character after a fast double-click (stale
  click), a Sold click could confirm a bid that changed a moment before, and a match where nobody can afford
  anything only moves by host gifts/skips through the whole remaining pool.

## Auction rules now (Step 4, `data/auction.js`)
Same as above, plus these fixes. All actions go through `ABC.auction.apply(state, action, ctx)` (pure; returns a new
state + structured events, or the unchanged state + a reason). The page only dispatches and renders.
- Money moves **only** on Sold: price = the confirmed high bid, deducted once; the second Sold has no bid to sell.
- Every button carries the round (and Sold carries the bid sequence). A click rendered for an earlier round or an
  older high bid is rejected (`stale`). Clicks within 300 ms of a round change are ignored (double-click guard).
- Bids/gifts to a full team are rejected; status shows **TEAM COMPLETE**. Duplicate canonical characters (same
  `charId`, any version) can't be assigned to either team.
- Gift: $0, recorded as a gift, counts full power, voids (does not charge) a pending bid.
- Player skip costs only that player's skip; master skip costs only a master skip and voids any pending bid.
- Every round change clears the bid. The match ends cleanly when: both teams are complete (`teams-complete`),
  the pool is exhausted (`pool-exhausted`), or **no player with an open slot has $1** (`no-buyers`, new: prevents
  endless host-only matches). Any action after the end is rejected. Winner rule unchanged.
- Rules live in `state.rules` (`startMoney 20, maxRoster 5, playerSkips 3, masterSkips 5, increments [1,2,5]`),
  so custom matches can change roster size etc. without code changes.
- Events: `start, reveal, bid{p,amt,prev}, sold{p,price,power}, gift{p,power,voided}, skip{kind player|master,p,voided},
  unsold, end{reason}` — each with `round` and entry `id`. `auction.rounds(state)` groups them for the history view;
  only revealed rounds appear, so upcoming characters are never exposed.

## Match setup (Step 5, `data/setup.js`)
- Settings object (versioned, `v:1`, saved as `recess-anime-auction-setup`): `mode, names, budget, team, skips, master,
  eligibility ('all'|'anime'), series (null = all), minStar, maxStar, version ('strongest'|'random'|'base'), draw`.
  `migrate()` upgrades older saves (v0 = only `prefs.mode`) and junk; `normalize()` clamps everything
  (budget 1–1000, team 1–10, skips 0–10, master 0–20, stars 1–7). Sound + reveal animations live in display prefs.
- Modes are patches over the settings, defined once in `SU.MODES`; all use the same auction engine:
  Classic (rules 20/5/3/5, 1–7★, balanced draw), Chaos (same rules, uniform draw), Elite (5–7★), Underdogs (1–4★),
  Custom. Editing a field a mode fixes switches to Custom.
- Pool: filter **versions** by series, eligibility (Anime only drops `anime:'not-shown'`) and star range, then pick one
  version per `charId` (strongest | random | base where `formType:'base'` is recorded — none recorded yet, so it falls
  back to strongest and says so). Counts are unique characters.
- Draw order: uniform = shuffle; balanced = shuffle per series, then deal series round-robin.
  **Rarity weighting and final selection algorithms are Step 7.**
- Validation: error if no series, min > max, or pool < 2 × team; warning if pool < 2 × team + all skips, if budget < team
  size, or if base forms are missing. Start is disabled with the message shown.
- A match copies the settings into `S.setup` and `S.rules` at start; later setup edits never change it.
- Anime only also hides detailed VS stats, abilities, notes, citations and source links during play
  (profiles include manga feats); the card shows only the animated form and game rating.

## Character selection (Step 7, `data/draw.js`, settings in `config.draw`)
1. **Eligibility** (`setup.pool`): playable (verified) versions only by default → series → Anime only (shown /
   anime-original versions) → star range → one version per canonical character by preference (strongest | random,
   resolved with the match seed | base where recorded). Every character holds exactly one ticket however many
   versions it has; `draw.order` throws if two versions of one character reach it.
2. **Order** fixed at match start from settings + seed + data snapshot; never shown; never reads money, scores,
   players or who is winning. Undo restores state including the order; encountered characters = reveal events.
   - **Balanced** (Classic, Elite, Underdogs, Custom "balanced"): weighted sampling without replacement. Each step
     picks a tier by `starWeights` {1:10, 2:15, 3:20, 4:25, 5:17, 6:10, 7:3 %} renormalised over tiers with characters
     left (tiers outside the range have none → their weight is redistributed); then a series inside that tier with
     weight `min(n^0.5, 1.5 × n × Σn^0.5 / N)` (n = its characters left in the tier) ÷ `(1 + 0.75 × (times seen −
     fewest seen))`, × 0.25 if it was the previous series and another is available; then a character, uniform except
     recently seen characters × 0.35.
   - **Uniform** (Chaos, Custom "uniform"): plain shuffle; no tier weights, no series balancing, no replay penalty.
3. **Replay variety**: characters revealed in completed matches go into a bounded history (300, newest first,
   `recess-anime-auction-recent`), used only by balanced draws, resettable in Settings.
4. **Snapshot**: a match stores power + star of every character it can draw (`S.snap`); saved/active matches keep
   their scores if data or scoring later changes. Winner calculation unchanged.
5. Performance: full 1,326-character order ≈ 1.6 ms; the page renders the 1,300-row list only while visible.

**Simulation** (`node tools/simulate.mjs`, 400 seeded matches × 30 draws = 12,000 draws + scenario runs):
Classic tiers 10.3/14.6/19.8/25.4/16.9/10.0/3.0 % vs target 10/15/20/25/17/10/3. Same series twice in a row 1.2 %
(6.3 % if drawn independently). Biggest series in the pool (One Piece 13 %, Dragon Ball 13 %) appear ~8 %; small ones are
lifted (Vinland Saga 0.5 % → 1.8 %). 1,300 of 1,326 characters seen. Chaos tiers match pool shares (±0.4 pt).
Elite 56.7/33.4/9.9 vs 56.7/33.3/10.0; Underdogs 14.3/21.5/28.4/35.9 vs 14.3/21.4/28.6/35.7; 6–7★ only 77.6/22.4 vs
76.9/23.1. Anime only (359) draws only shown versions. One series (One Piece) and a 7-character pool (Vinland Saga,
fully drawn 200×) work. Random versions only pick eligible versions. Recent penalty: recent characters fall from
19.8 % to 9.8 % of Classic draws, unchanged in Chaos. No duplicates; order independent of database insertion order.
**Known deviation**: 1★ characters appear more often per character (busiest ~19 % of matches) because the 10 % target
share is spread over only 29 playable 1★ characters — a consequence of the weights, not a bug. The first simulation
showed lone small-series characters boosted up to ~5×; fixed with the 1.5× `maxCharacterBoost` cap.

**Scoring audit** (`node tools/score-audit.mjs`, read-only): medians rise monotonically across all 41 subtiers; 0.6 %
of neighbouring-subtier pairs invert (secondary stats, by design). Within a subtier scores barely differ because the
1–100 integer scale gives each subtier ~1 point and secondary stats ≤ 20 % of a band: e.g. High 6-A 137 entries / 4
distinct scores; 2-C 104 entries / 2 scores (mostly Dragon Ball). Conditional tiers 566, hedged 131, hax-heavy 122,
characters with versions in several continuities 18 (kept as separate versions, never merged), VS-outdated 41.
**Not changed**: altering the formula would rescore every character; decision recorded below.

## Optional features (Step 8)
- **Undo** (button / U): whole-state snapshots, newest 40 (`session.pushUndo`). Restores balances, rosters, gift/price
  records, skips, master skips, round, bid, leader, events/history, encountered characters (from reveal events),
  order + seed (so the upcoming sequence never changes) and the timer (restored paused). Disabled when empty.
  Mystery: undo that would hide an already revealed character is refused with a message.
- **Autosave** after every change to `recess-anime-auction-match` (save v5: state + undo). State includes setup,
  seed, order of version ids, score snapshot, rosters, bid, events, timer, pause. Load → Resume / Discard banner on the
  setup screen (never auto-replaced; starting a new match asks first). Corrupt, old (v4) or unknown-character saves are
  cleared with a message. Back to setup keeps the match saved.
- **Blind** preset (`info:'blind'`, Classic rules): identity, art, series and form shown; power, VS tier, stats, team
  power, power bar, list hidden; stars optional (`blindStars`). Everything is revealed at the end.
- **Mystery** preset (`info:'mystery'`): identity, art, form, power hidden until a sale or gift reveals the character;
  clues (series, power class from stars, fighting style from the entry's VS ability names, stars, role from VS page
  categories) each switchable; "Not recorded" when the data has none. Skipped characters stay hidden until the end.
  Both modes state that hiding is for local play (developer tools can read the data).
- **Timer** (off by default; 10/15/30/60 s): starts on each reveal; after a bid reset / +5 s / keep running; at zero
  "sell to the high bidder" (never without a bid — then the host decides) or "always wait". Time is computed from
  wall-clock deltas; pause stops it; the round/sequence tokens stop a double resolution with a manual Sold.
- **Pause / Resume** (button / P): blocks auction actions, freezes the timer, overlay with Resume.
- **Match controls**: Rematch (same setup, new seed and characters; confirm if unfinished), Setup (keeps match saved),
  Play again at the end, Rules, Settings. Confirmations use an in-page dialog.
- **Full screen** (button / F): native Fullscreen API on the stage; fallback "focus mode" hides page chrome when the
  API is missing or refused (Esc / F leaves).
- **Shortcuts** (Settings toggle, on by default; ? lists them): 1/2/3 and 8/9/0 bid +1/+2/+5 for each player,
  Shift+S confirm sale, U undo, P pause, V stats, F full screen. Ignored while typing, in dialogs, with modifier keys
  or key repeat. No shortcut for gifts, skips, all-in or discarding.
- **Audio**: synthesized tones (no files): bid, sale, gift, skip, reveal, high-rarity reveal (not in Mystery/no-star
  Blind), timer ticks (last 3 s), victory. Mute = Sound effects toggle; volume slider in Settings; created only after
  a user action; errors swallowed.

## Results (Step 9, `data/results.js`)
- At match end `summarize()` builds a self-contained summary (stored in `S.summary` and in the bounded local history
  `recess-anime-auction-history`, 50 matches, newest first, same match id replaces). It copies every character's
  name, series, form, stars, power (from the match snapshot) and VS tier, so stored results never change when the
  database does. Undoing a finished match removes its history entry until it finishes again.
- Winner = existing rule (`auction.rank`): total power → strongest single character → money left → draw. Shown as
  "won by N power", "decided by the existing tiebreak" or "Draw". No bonus points anywhere.
- Screen (full width, replaces the arena): winner banner (gold accent, one 0.45 s entrance, reduced-motion safe,
  victory tone if sound is on), final scores, power share bar (0 + 0 → even), totals (revealed, auctions, bids,
  gifts, skips, master skips), actions (Rematch, Match setup, Full auction history, Previous matches), both teams
  (power, spent, left, bought, gifted, skips used; compact cards with portrait, series · form, stars, VS tier,
  power, price/GIFT, click for details), awards, collapsible statistics, collapsible full history with filters
  (All, each player, Purchases, Gifts, Skips).
- Awards (only shown when eligible data exists; ties list every holder and are marked "tie"): Match MVP (highest
  power acquired), Best Bargain (purchased power ÷ price), Most Expensive Purchase, Most Efficient Bidder (purchased
  power ÷ money spent; gifts excluded; players who spent $0 excluded), Biggest Bidding War (sold lot with most bids,
  needs ≥ 2), Most Legendary Team (most characters at 6★+, `HIGH_TIER_MIN`), Best Gift.
- Statistics per player: power min/avg/max, star distribution mini-bars, spending, average price, purchased power
  per $, power from gifts, highest purchase, series on team, bids, auctions won, unused skips; skipped characters and
  unused master skips.
- Blind/Mystery: everything (including skipped characters) is revealed on the results screen. Anime only: live
  details still use the spoiler-safe panel; previous matches show only the stored fields.
- Previous matches dialog: list, full read-only results per match (details "as recorded"), filters, Clear history
  (confirmed).

## Database expansion (Step 6)
- 19 series in the central registry (`config.series`: VS category, canonical origins, fully-animated origins,
  manga-only origins, medium). Coverage numbers live in the generated `DATABASE_PROGRESS.md`.
- `node tools/build-data.mjs --series <ids|all>` imports a batch: category members → pages (50 per API request,
  cached in `tools/cache/`, gitignored) → canonical character pages only (origin whitelist; live-action/games/films,
  items and non-profiles excluded) → names normalised, versions grouped under one `charId` → latest key (+ "Base"
  key) extracted → scored → status → `tools/imports/<series>.json` → merged with the reviewer spec and validated.
  Unchanged revisions are reused (`IMPORTER_V` bump forces re-processing). Manifest: `tools/imports/manifest.json`.
- Status: `verified` + `review.by: 'reviewer'` (17 Step-3 versions) or `'auto'` (machine-verified: unambiguous tier,
  key and canonical origin from the cited revision; not human-reviewed). Anything ambiguous (Unknown, Varies,
  ranges, estimates, key/value count mismatches, failed extraction) and unmatched legacy entries go to
  `needs-review` and are **not playable** unless the setup's "Include unreviewed characters" is on.
- Anime status from the registry: fully-animated origins → `shown`, anime-original origins → `anime-only`,
  manga-only origins → `not-shown`, otherwise `unknown` (excluded from Anime only).
- New schema values: anime status `unknown`, medium `novel` (Classroom of the Elite). New validation: canonical origin,
  manga-only marked as shown, image URL host, versions of one `charId` sharing name and series, possible duplicates.
- The character list renders only while visible (IntersectionObserver) — 1,300+ rows.

## Known limitations
- Machine-verified entries (1,309 characters) were checked by rules, not by a person; 306 versions need manual review.
- 967 playable characters have unconfirmed anime status, so Anime only has 359 characters.
- Artwork exists for only the original roster (AniList hotlinks, licence unconfirmed); new characters use fallback art.
- Within a VS subtier scores barely differ (integer 1–100 scale; see score audit). Changing it rescores everything.
- Only each profile's latest key (+ "Base") is imported; earlier versions can be added per character.
- Hidden-information modes don't protect against reading the page data in developer tools.
- Phones: header buttons wrap to two rows; the second row of bid buttons may need a short scroll on 390×844.
- Manual verification still needed: real touch devices, a screen reader pass, real browser full-screen on Safari,
  sound on mobile browsers, and spot checks of machine-verified characters against VS Battles.
