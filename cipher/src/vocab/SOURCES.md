# CIPHER vocabulary — sources and licenses

The bank (`data/bank.json`) is generated by `scripts/vocab/build.ts` (`npm run vocab`). It contains **no official Codenames word list**. Downloads are cached in `scripts/vocab/.cache/`, which is git-ignored.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Princeton WordNet 3.0** (`WNdb-3.0.tar.gz`, wordnetcode.princeton.edu) | General, Objects & occupations, Animals & nature, Geography, Science & history, Advanced. Also lexical categories, sense counts (polysemy), SemCor tag counts and usage-domain flags. | WordNet 3.0 license (permissive, attribution). | **Wikidata** (query.wikidata.org, 8 SPARQL queries) | Single-word anime/manga works and characters, video games, characters and franchises, films, TV series and screen characters, ranked by sitelink count. | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | 2026-11-27 |
| **Cipher original lists** (`scripts/vocab/curated/*.txt`) | Anime, gaming and screen terms; filler-word stoplist; safety blocklist. | Original work for Cipher | 2026-11-27 |

## How entries are chosen
- **Shape:** single words, letters only, 3–12 characters. No acronyms, no 3-letter proper-name abbreviations, no stopwords.
- **WordNet:** nouns and verbs only, and words that are mainly adjectives are dropped.
  - Each word's category comes from the lexical file of its most-tagged sense.
  - Recognizability score: `2.2·ln(1+SemCor tags) + ln(1+mentions in WordNet glosses) + 0.6·ln(senses)`, with a small penalty for length over 9. The polysemy term rewards words with several meanings.
  - Each category keeps its best-scoring words up to its target. Advanced takes the best leftover words of 6+ letters.
  - Proper names outside geography must appear in at least 3 other WordNet definitions.
- **Wikidata:** titles and characters by sitelink count. When a title is already a general word (FROZEN, ALIEN), the existing entry gains a pack tag instead of a duplicate.
- **Difficulty:** by rank within the category (top 45% easy, next 40% medium, rest hard; Advanced 30% medium, 70% hard). Original curated terms are easy.
- **Root / variation group:** `stem()` from `engine/clues.ts`, the same rule the clue validator uses.

## Safety
- **Not imported:** WordNet usage domains *obscenity, ethnic slur, disparagement, slang, euphemism, vulgarism*; *archaism, plural, combining form, trademark* are skipped as well.
- **Blocklist:** `curated/blocklist.txt` covers words and their root groups: sexual, slurs, drugs, graphic violence, bodily functions, and identity terms (so they can't be used to target players).
- **`sensitive`:** religious figures and terms, found by definition keywords. These are kept for review but **never drawn**.
- This is automated curation. Manual review passes are still recommended, especially for the long tail of Wikidata characters and WordNet hard words.
